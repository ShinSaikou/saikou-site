# History and statistics

## The History tab

**History** lists the entries you have read, most recent first, each with the last chapter you
opened.

- Tap a row to resume reading: it opens that chapter where you left off, or the next chapter once
  you have finished it. When there is none yet, Saikou says **There's no next chapter yet**.
- Tap the cover to open the entry's page.
- The delete button, the row's only button, removes the entry from the history (**Entry removed
  from history**).
- **Search** filters the list by title.
- Long press a row (or its cover) to select it, tap others to add them, then **Remove** them
  (**Remove N entries from history?**).
- **Hide NSFW entries** in the top bar leaves out the entries whose source comes from an installed
  extension marked NSFW, search results included, and the same button then reads **Show NSFW
  entries**. Entries from the Local source, or from a source with no installed extension, always
  stay. When that leaves nothing to show, the tab says **NSFW entries are hidden.**
- **Clear all** in the top bar (the sweep icon) empties the whole history after a confirmation:
  "Every entry leaves the history. Library entries and read chapters stay as they are." It clears
  every entry, hidden ones too.

Removing history never changes which chapters are marked read and never removes anything from your
library.

## Incognito

**Incognito** in the More tab's **Quick controls** stops Saikou from writing reading history while
it is on. It turns itself off the next time the app starts fresh.

For one extension's sources only, open the extension's page from Browse > Extensions and turn on
**Incognito** there ("Do not record history for this extension's sources"). That one stays on until
you turn it off.

**Secure screen** can follow incognito: see [Security and privacy](security-and-privacy.md).

## Statistics

More > **Statistics** sums up your library:

- **Overview**: entries **In library**, total **Read duration** and **Completed entries**.
- **Entries**: how many are **In global update** (included in library updates), **Started** and
  **Local**.
- **Chapters**: **Total**, **Read** and **Downloaded**.
- **Trackers**: **Tracked entries**, your **Mean score** and the trackers **Used**.
