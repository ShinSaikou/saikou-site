Sync between devices

Sync keeps your library the same on your phone, tablet and any other device running Saikou. Read a chapter on one and the others know. This is one of Saikou's own features.

Sync goes through storage you already own: your Google Drive, or a WebDAV server. Saikou has no server of its own.

What sync carries#

  • Your library entries, with each entry's notes, manual order, reading mode and chapter list settings, and the details you edited.
  • Categories.
  • Chapter progress (read and unread, page reached), reading history, bookmarks and page notes. Marking a chapter unread and removing a bookmark reach your other devices too.
  • Tracking links.
  • Each entry's scanlator filter and group ranking.
  • Merged entries.

What stays on each device: settings, extensions and their repositories, source settings, tracker sign-ins and downloaded chapters. Install the extensions you need on each device.

Setting it up#

Open More > Settings > Sync and choose a Sync service.

Google Drive#

  1. Choose Google Drive and tap Sign in with Google.
  2. Google's sign-in page opens in your browser. Sign in and approve, then tap Return to Saikou on the page that follows, or the notification.
  3. Back in Saikou, the Google account row shows the account.

Saikou asks only for access to its own hidden app folder in your Drive. It cannot see or change your other files, and the folder does not appear in your Drive's file list.

A Google sign-in does not expire. If you signed in during testing, before 24 October 2026, Saikou still shows an end date for that sign-in: "Google's sign-in for this device expires on ...". A day before that date Saikou posts a notification, Google sign-in for sync ends soon; tap Sign in again to open the sync settings, then tap Sign in with Google again once and the date goes away for good. Signing in again with the same account continues where the device left off.

The Google Drive choice appears when this build of Saikou offers it.

WebDAV#

For your own server or a storage provider that offers WebDAV.

  1. Choose WebDAV.
  2. Enter the Server address (it must start with https://), your User name and Password.
  3. Tap Sign in.

Saikou only accepts certificates your device trusts, and never sends your library over plain http://. If the server redirects to another address, Saikou says so; enter the final address instead.

When sync runs#

  • On the schedule set in Automatic sync: Off, 1 h, 6 h (the default), 12 h or 24 h.
  • When you open the app, if the last successful sync is more than a few minutes old.
  • When you leave the app with changes not yet sent.
  • Shortly after you close the reader.
  • When you tap Sync now.

If background syncs do not run, tap Allow background sync (shown until Saikou may ignore battery optimisation).

Last sync shows when the last run finished, or when and why it failed, with the last successful sync below it.

When a background sync stops for a reason you can fix, Saikou posts a Sync stopped notification saying why: the device lost its sign-in, Google no longer accepts it, the server refused the saved password, or Google Drive is full. Tap it (or Sign in again / Open sync settings) to go to the sync settings. Each reason is posted once, and the notification goes away after the next successful sync. A sync that failed because the network or the server was down posts nothing: the next sync tries again. A sync you start with Sync now shows its error under Last sync instead.

A running sync and these notices have their own notification channels (Sync progress and Sync errors), so you can silence one in Android's settings without the other.

The first sync#

The first time a device syncs with an account or server, it pulls everything already there, merges it into its own library, then uploads its whole library once. After that, each sync sends and receives only what changed.

When two devices change the same thing between syncs, both end up with the same result:

  • A chapter's read mark and its bookmark take the most recent change. Mark a chapter unread on one device and it is unread on the others after their next sync; the same goes for a bookmark you remove.
  • The page reached in a chapter takes the side that got further.
  • Settings you choose on an entry (its categories, reading mode, notes, edited details) take the most recent change.
  • Details that come from the source are settled by the same rule on every device, so both end up with the same copy.

The safety backup#

Before a device's first sync, Saikou writes a backup of that device's library into its automatic backup folder, so nothing can be lost by joining. The Sync section names the file. Saikou keeps it until two later sign-ins have each written a newer one.

The notice in More > Settings > Sync ends with Save a copy, which opens Android's save dialog and copies the file wherever you choose. The file is also listed on the Restore tab of More > Backup, under Automatic backups, as a Sync safety backup, to restore or copy. With a Storage location set, the automatic backup folder is its autobackup folder, which you can open yourself. See Storage location.

Sync reports#

  • Last sync report shows the library before and after the run (entries, categories, read chapters, bookmarks), how many routine merges there were, and any conflicts: changes made on this device that met a change from another device, each naming the entry and the fields where the other device's value won.
  • Earlier runs keeps the reports of previous syncs.

Checking that two devices agree#

Library fingerprint: tap it to compute a short code from everything this device syncs. Two devices that have both finished syncing show the same code. If they differ, tap Sync now on both and compare again.

Signing out#

Sign out ("Stop syncing this device") forgets the account or server details on this device. Your library on this device and Saikou's files in your Drive or on your server stay as they are. For Google Drive, signing out also revokes Saikou's access to your account.

If you sign in again later, this device joins as a new device: it pulls everything from that account or server, then uploads its whole library once. A different Google account, server or user name works the same way.

Sync format#

Saikou 0.1.5 syncs in a new format, the one that carries unread marks and removed bookmarks. Once one device runs 0.1.5, every device that syncs with it needs 0.1.5 to keep syncing. Until it is updated, an older device says Update Saikou on this device to keep syncing, and it syncs again once it runs 0.1.5.

One case still brings a read mark back, with Merged entries on. When another device has a source in a merged title that this device lacks, and holds that source's copy of a chapter as read, the chapter can come back as read here after a sync.

Messages you may see#

  • Update Saikou on this device to keep syncing: another device wrote a newer sync format. See Sync format.
  • Signed out of the sync server. Sign in again to keep syncing. / The server refused the saved password.: enter the password again; the device keeps its place on the server.
  • Google no longer accepts this device's sign-in: tap Sign in with Google again.
  • Google Drive is full: free some space in the Google account, then sync again.
  • The sync server did not answer: check that the server is running and reachable from this device.
  • The backup Saikou writes before the first sync could not be written: nothing was synced. Check free space on the device, then sync again.
