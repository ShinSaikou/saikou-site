Saikou documentation

Saikou is an open-source manga reader for Android. Releases and the source code are on GitHub. These pages describe the app as it is built today (version 0.0.1).

Saikou brings no content of its own. You read what is on your device through the Local source, or add extensions from repositories you choose.

Screens and settings are named the way the app labels them. A path such as More > Settings > Downloads means: open the More tab, tap Settings, then tap Downloads.

Getting started#

  • Getting started: install, first launch, add a repository and an extension, add your first entry, bring your library over from another reader.

Guides#

  • Library and categories: the Library tab, categories and sub-categories, filters, sorting, display and selecting many entries at once.
  • An entry's page: chapters, chapter filters and sorting, editing the title and description, custom covers, per-entry reading mode.
  • Library updates and the Updates tab: automatic and manual updates, smart update, update errors, notifications.
  • History and statistics: reading history, incognito, the Statistics screen.
  • Browse and sources: the Sources tab, pinned sources, global search, source filters, source settings and cookies.
  • Extensions and repositories: adding repositories, installing, trusting, updating and uninstalling extensions.
  • Downloads: downloading chapters, the download queue, automatic downloads and deletes, storage, copying downloads from another reader.
  • The reader: reading modes, the reader's settings sheet, page actions, bookmarks and page notes.
  • Tracking: MyAnimeList and AniList, linking entries, and comparing your list with the app.
  • Backups and restore: creating, scheduling and restoring backups, including ones from other readers.
  • Sync between devices: Google Drive and WebDAV sync, what it carries, sync reports.
  • Migration: moving entries to another source without losing progress.
  • The Local source: reading files on your device, folder layout, metadata and covers.
  • Security and privacy: app lock, secure screen, notifications, incognito, and what Saikou sends over the network.
  • Settings reference: every section of More > Settings, row by row.

Help#

  • Frequently asked questions
  • Troubleshooting: network and site errors, WebView, browser checks, crash logs.
